The collective is one organisation made of houses. Each house has its own website and api, its own voice, and its own Arabic word-sign, but they share one brand, one contributor portal and one sign-in.

| House | Arabic | Sense |
|---|---|---|
| Collective | جماعة · *jamāʿa* | the collective: the hub everything turns around |
| AI | ذكاء · *dhakāʾ* | intelligence |
| Engineering | هندسة · *handasa* | engineering, and also geometry |
| Health | عافية · *ʿāfiya* | wellbeing: soundness of body and soul |
| Institute | معهد · *maʿhad* | institute |
| Open Source | مشاع · *mashāʿ* | the commons |
| Research | بحث · *baḥth* | inquiry |

> [!NOTE]
> At launch, the contributor portal offers the **Collective** and **Open Source** houses. The others exist and keep their history, and open as their programmes do.

## Services every house shares

Alongside the houses run services that belong to no single house:

- **Portal**: where contributors sign in, claim tasks and see their work across the collective.
- **Accounts**: [Sign in with ITC](/platform/sign-in-with-itc), the collective's identity provider.
- **CMS**: the content service behind every house's website. Staff sign in to every staff tool with their CMS account.
- **Email**: lists, campaigns and transactional mail for every house.
- **[Status](/platform/status)**, **[Analytics](/platform/analytics)** and **[Errors](/platform/errors)**: the collective's own uptime pages, privacy-first analytics and error tracker.
- **Docs**: these pages.
