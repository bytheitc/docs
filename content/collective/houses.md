The collective is one organisation made of houses. Each house has its own website, its own voice and its own Arabic word-sign, and they share one brand and one contributor portal.

| House | Arabic | Sense |
|---|---|---|
| Collective | جماعة · *jamāʿa* | the collective: the hub everything turns around |
| Open Source | مشاع · *mashāʿ* | the commons |

## Services every house shares

- **Portal**: where contributors sign in, claim tasks and see their work across the collective.
- **CMS**: the content service behind every house's website.
- **Email**: lists, campaigns and transactional mail.
- **[Errors](/platform/errors)**: the collective's own error tracker.
